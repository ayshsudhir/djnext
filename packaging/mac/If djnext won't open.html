If djnext won't open

Your Mac says "djnext" Not Opened. That's expected: djnext is a free app that isn't registered with Apple. Click Done on that message (not Move to Trash), then use one of these two ways. You only need to do this once.

Way 1 · clicks

Allow it in System Settings

  1. First drag djnext into Applications and try to open it once (that's when the Mac blocks it).
  2. Press this button. It opens the right page of System Settings:

    Open Privacy & Security
    If your browser asks whether to open System Settings, click Allow.
  3. Scroll down to Security. Next to "djnext" was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway.
  4. Click Open Anyway again and enter your Mac password. djnext opens.

No Open Anyway button? It only shows for about an hour after the Mac blocked djnext. Open djnext again, click Done, then press the button above.

Way 2 · one line

Or copy one line into Terminal

  1. Make sure djnext is in your Applications folder.
  2. Press Copied ✓
  3. Open Terminal: press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, press Enter.
  4. Click in the Terminal window, press ⌘ V to paste, then Enter. djnext opens.
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/djnext.app && open /Applications/djnext.app

What it does: removes the "downloaded from the internet" mark from djnext only, then opens it. It doesn't change any other setting on your Mac.

Still stuck? Ask on the djnext page or message whoever sent you djnext.
