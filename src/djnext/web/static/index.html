    How do I find my SoundCloud link?
    1. Open SoundCloud ↗ and sign in. It opens in your web browser.
    2. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then click Profile.
    3. Click the address bar at the top of the browser, press ⌘C to copy the address, come back here and press ⌘V to paste it below.

    It looks like https://soundcloud.com/your-name. djnext reads your public likes and playlists (only the track lists, nothing is downloaded) and suggests only those tracks that are also in your rekordbox Collection. Skip this and djnext uses your whole rekordbox Collection instead.

    Make more tracks ready

    djnext can only suggest a track after rekordbox has analysed it (tempo, key, structure). Tracks in your SoundCloud lists that aren't in your rekordbox Collection yet can't be used. You can add a whole list in one go:

    1. Open rekordbox. In the left sidebar, click SoundCloud, then the list (for example Likes). You need to be logged in to SoundCloud in rekordbox, with a SoundCloud Go+ or DJ plan.
    2. Click any track in the list, then press ⌘A to select them all.
    3. Drag the selected tracks onto Collection at the top of the left sidebar.
    4. Wait until rekordbox has finished analysing them (the progress bar at the top disappears).
    5. Come back here and press Reload library.

    Lists you don't DJ with (for example a rock playlist)? Switch off Use for DJing above and they stop counting.

    Tip: better energy

    Star-rate tracks in rekordbox (1★ chill … 5★ peak-time). Without ratings, djnext guesses energy from tempo and how much of each track is drops.

    Welcome to djnext

    djnext works with the tracks in your rekordbox Collection that rekordbox has analysed (downloaded files or streamed tracks). It puts the tracks in an order that flows, shows you when and how to mix each one, and can put the set straight into rekordbox with hot cues on your pads.
